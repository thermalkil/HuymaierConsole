Huymaier Console v0.24.6
PS2 Audio Sequences, PS3 Home Ambience and Installer Fix

INSTALLER
- Fixes the v0.24.5 compile failure where a PS2 memory-card mode constant shadowed System.IO.FileMode.

PLAYSTATION 2 AUDIO
- Adds an importable startup MP3 that plays once when the PS2 interface opens.
- The startup track automatically hands off to the imported looping menu ambience.
- Adds an importable game-selection boot sound that finishes before PCSX2 launches.
- Startup and game-boot audio can be enabled independently and use the boot/sound-effects volume.
- Existing navigation, confirm and back sounds remain configurable.

PLAYSTATION 3 AUDIO
- Includes a Home Menu introduction made from the supplied 003. Home Menu.mp3.
- The introduction transitions into a softened ambient background loop.
- The ambient loop uses a crossfaded boundary so it repeats without a hard restart.
- Choosing custom XMB music still overrides the included sequence; Use Included Home Menu Audio restores it.

PRESERVED
- Keeps the PS2 saved-data browser, #-Z library index, PCSX2 Big Picture overlay, cover reuse, launch fixes, PS3 themes and all v0.23.8 stability work.
