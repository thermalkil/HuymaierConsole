# Huymaier Console v0.30.6

This release promotes the fully validated v0.30.6 RC8 candidate and expands the console 3D-model editor into a full per-console presentation editor.

## Full console model editing

- **Edit Model** now supports per-console **Yaw, Pitch, Roll, Scale, Position X, Position Y, Mirror X, Mirror Y, Mirror Z, face mode, lighting, and fan-motion strength**.
- Per-console scale supports **30% to 300%** in exact 10% steps.
- Per-console lighting supports **20% to 200%**.
- Face handling supports **Normal, Reverse, and Two-Sided** presentation modes for imported GLB assets with unusual winding/orientation.
- Mirror controls allow correction of models whose lettering or geometry is genuinely reversed without modifying the source GLB.
- Saved presentation values are persisted independently for each console model and apply to both its shelf card and full-screen viewer.
- Provider models remain isolated from console-only presentation adjustments.

## 3D renderer and model handling

- Carries forward HC3D v4 and glTF `COLOR_0` support so vertex-colored models retain their authored colors.
- Carries forward adaptive transformed-normal winding repair and mirrored-node tangent-handness preservation.
- Keeps bounded fan-style idle motion instead of unrestricted full rotation.
- Preserves textured, base-color-only, emissive, double-sided, mirrored, and textureless material paths.

## Customization and platform stability

- Carries forward overall Huymaier Console brightness control from **0% to 200%** in exact 10% increments.
- Carries forward the repaired Windows/Xbox Full Screen Experience updater handoff.
- Preserves controller navigation, Quick Access, Downloads, Recomps, GameCube, streaming, provider integrations, Nintendo ownership filtering, and installer integrity protections.
