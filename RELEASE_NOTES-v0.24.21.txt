Huymaier Console v0.24.21
Xbox Dashboard Reconstruction, Safe Style Switching, Achievements and Save Management

Xbox interfaces
- Reconstructs Original Xbox as a dedicated 5960/TeamUIX-style green orb dashboard rather than a generic launcher panel.
- Reconstructs Xbox 360 Blades with four expanding blades, metallic blade borders, page-colored interiors, gamertag/Gamerscore header, two-pane Games blade, and true blade switching.
- Reconstructs Xbox 360 Metro with Home, Social, Games, Video, Music, Apps, and Settings hubs, a profile header, action rail, and variable-size tiles.
- TeamUIX, XBMC360, and DashX360 are references only. Huymaier Console no longer detects or launches those UI applications. Game selections launch directly through xemu or Xenia.

Dashboard stability
- Dashboard-style changes are now guarded in-place swaps: old subpages and Guide state are cleared, held input is neutralized, the target page is selected before rebuilding, and the host window is explicitly restored.
- A failed switch rolls back without closing Xbox 360 or Huymaier Console and writes a diagnostic line.

Xbox 360 achievements
- Reads original Xbox 360 achievements from Xenia Canary profile GPD/XDBF files.
- Shows title, achievement name/description, earned state, Gamerscore, secret behavior, unlock time, and embedded achievement image where present.
- The Blades Xbox Live blade, Metro Social/Games hubs, and Xbox Guide use the same profile achievement data.

Xbox saves
- Xbox 360 scans Xenia content roots and exposes title save containers with size/date, backup, export, open-location, and recoverable removal.
- Original Xbox detects xemu hard-disk and memory-unit images and provides image-level backup/export/recoverable removal without pretending individual FATX saves were parsed.
- Removed items are moved into Huymaier Console backups rather than permanently deleted.
